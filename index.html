4. ZATCA, SABER & Gulf Customs Compliance Protocols

Commercial consignments entering the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia are subject to the Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority (ZATCA) regulations and the SASO SABER electronic platform:

  • Personal Import Exemption Threshold: Consignments valued under 1,000 SAR (~$266 USD) enter free of customs duty, subject only to 15% standard Value Added Tax (VAT).
  • Commercial Shipments & CR Mandate: Importers must possess an active Commercial Registration (CR) and link their Harmonized System (HS) codes through the SABER system to generate Product and Shipment Certificates of Conformity (PCoC / SCoC).
  • Medical & Food Products (SFDA): Pharmaceutical, supplement, and cosmetic consignments require Saudi Food and Drug Authority pre-clearance or individual personal-use prescription validation.
